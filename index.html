The Omnic Forge

What is the
Omnic Forge?

The Omnic Forge is the first of it's kind AI. We use computer vision and deep learning techniques to analyze video of your gameplay to help you improve your playstyle. As our community grows, so does the Forge's knowledge of the game helping you to get better.

Learn More

What kinds of information does the Omnic Forge provide?

Omnic provides personalized AI coaching insights, an automatic generated highlight video, advanced analytics, aim analysis, comparisons to friends, rivals, and pros. More and more features are being added all of the time so sign up today.

Getting started

How easy is it to use the Omnic Forge?

Simply connect your Twitch or YouTube Gaming account and, whenever you stream, Forge will be watching and analyze your games that it understands. After every match you will automatically receive a post match report. If you don't like to stream you can also upload videos or link to a video that you have already posted on the web.
