Second area — beside the homepage

A shared design system, documented once

The homepage exploration is one page. Omnic's public presence is more than one page — and the moment a visitor clicks Sign Up Now or Pricing, they leave omnic.ai entirely and land on forge.omnic.ai. I looked at what happens across that whole surface, not just the one page I rebuilt.

Observation

Across omnic.ai's other pages and the entry points into the Forge app, the same building blocks — a page header, a primary button, a toggle — get re-implemented slightly differently each time, rather than drawn from one shared spec.

Opportunity

The brand's colour palette clearly does travel between the marketing site and the product app. The components built on top of that palette don't yet — which is the situation where things drift a little further apart every time a page is added.

Hypothesis

A small, documented set of tokens and the handful of controls that actually repeat could let new pages and screens draw from one source instead of each hand-tuning its own — something I'd want to confirm by watching how a page actually gets built today, not something I can claim from outside.

Artifact

This page — the token system and components from the homepage prototype, extracted into a standalone, labelled reference. Not a rebuild of any additional page or of the Forge app.

What I found

The same job, several different implementations

Measured directly from the live pages — computed styles and DOM inspection, not a guess. Two things stood out: the page-header band that opens About, Careers, Community and Partnerships is hand-set per page rather than driven by one scale, and the primary-action button renders four different ways across just two screens of the sign-up flow on forge.omnic.ai.

Components

The controls that actually repeat

Three button treatments, because three is what the live surfaces actually use — a filled primary action, a restrained secondary, and an outline variant modelled directly on the real OAuth buttons at forge.omnic.ai/users/sign_up. This page documents a proposal, built from the same tokens and components as the homepage exploration — it is not a rebuild. About, Careers, Community, Partnerships, and the Forge product app were not modified; I don't have access to the Forge codebase, and rebuilding a live signed-in product was outside what this exploration set out to do. What's here is scoped to be the reference those pages could draw from, if that turned out to be useful.
